WorthBook helps you track total net worth, assets vs liabilities, and day-to-day ledger entries — entirely on your device.

• Home dashboard with net worth trends and account list
• Accounts for cash, investments, property, crypto, loans, and more
• Ledger for income and expenses (optionally linked to accounts)
• Insights charts: growth, allocation, cashflow, and categories
• Multi-currency with manual exchange rates
• JSON / CSV export and biometric lock (Face ID / Touch ID)
• No sign-in, no cloud sync, no ads — your data stays private

Perfect for offline-first wealth tracking on iPhone and iPad.
