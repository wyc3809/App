# Art Direction

Jianghu Life uses premium original wuxia visual direction.

## Core Style

- Ink wash
- Rice paper
- Deep ink black
- Warm ivory
- Muted cinnabar red
- Restrained antique gold
- Jade accents
- Wood and cloth textures
- Subtle motion

## Avoid

- Generic AI fantasy
- Sci-fi interface language
- Cheap mobile RPG gloss
- Overuse of glow and gradients
- Copyrighted characters, factions, techniques, locations, or visual likenesses

## Asset Policy

Every asset must be recorded in `AssetsSource/Licenses/ASSET_REGISTER.md`.
